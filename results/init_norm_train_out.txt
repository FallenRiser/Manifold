SECTION 3: Why normalisation reduces init sensitivity
==========================================================================
The thesis as a number: how much does the CHOICE of init still matter
once BatchNorm is in the network? Depth 8, width 32, 3 seeds.

      init     no BN   with BN
------------------------------
     0.001    0.4700    0.9500
      0.01    0.4700    0.9500
    xavier    0.9450    0.9433
        he    0.9383    0.9467
       1.0    0.8767    0.9467
       5.0    0.8433    0.9417
------------------------------
    spread    0.4750    0.0083

The spread across init scales is what 'init sensitivity' means.
Shrinking it is what normalisation is FOR.
